### OpenSourcePOS — Gift Card Payment Validation

Credited as the reporter of [GHSA-9847-7w5f-cg9r](https://github.com/opensourcepos/opensourcepos/security/advisories/GHSA-9847-7w5f-cg9r), a payment validation mismatch that let a cashier submit a negative gift-card amount, increasing balances and creating store credit.

**High · CVSS 7.1 · CWE-20**

Published October 2, 2026 · Affected through 3.4.1 · Fixed in 3.4.2

### OpenSourcePOS — Reports Permission Bypass

Credited as the reporter of [GHSA-9gr6-4mm4-4wrq](https://github.com/opensourcepos/opensourcepos/security/advisories/GHSA-9gr6-4mm4-4wrq), which allowed authenticated employees without the required report permissions to access reports through double URL encoding.

**Moderate · CVSS 6.5 · CWE-177**

Published October 2, 2026 · Affected through 3.4.1 · Fixed in 3.4.2

### OpenSourcePOS — Stored XSS in Item Tax Name

Credited as the reporter of [GHSA-92cx-fc8x-7wmm](https://github.com/opensourcepos/opensourcepos/security/advisories/GHSA-92cx-fc8x-7wmm), a stored XSS vulnerability caused by item tax names being saved without sanitization and rendered unescaped in several views.

**Moderate · CVSS 5.4 · CWE-79**

Published October 2, 2026 · Affected through 3.4.1 · Fixed in 3.4.2

### OpenSourcePOS — Reflected XSS in Item Kit Barcode Generation

**Moderate · CVSS 4.8 · CWE-79**
